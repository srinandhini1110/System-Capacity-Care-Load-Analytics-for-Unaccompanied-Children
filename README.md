# System Capacity & Care Load Analytics for Unaccompanied Children

## Project Overview

This project analyzes operational trends in the HHS Unaccompanied Children Program using official data from the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS). The objective is to understand care load patterns, identify trends in child apprehensions and discharges, and generate data-driven insights to support resource planning and decision-making.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze trends in children receiving HHS care.
- Study apprehension, transfer, and discharge patterns.
- Identify monthly and yearly care load trends.
- Examine relationships between operational metrics.
- Provide recommendations for improving system capacity planning.

---

## Dataset

Source: U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS)

Dataset Name:
HHS Unaccompanied Children Program

Records: 1,314

Features:
- Date
- Children Apprehended and Placed in CBP Custody
- Children in CBP Custody
- Children Transferred out of CBP Custody
- Children in HHS Care
- Children Discharged from HHS Care

## Key Insights

- HHS care demand changes significantly over time.
- Apprehensions strongly influence the number of children entering care.
- Monthly trends indicate seasonal variations in workload.
- High-demand periods can be identified for improved resource planning.
- Operational metrics are positively correlated, supporting forecasting efforts.

---

## Recommendations

- Develop predictive models for future care demand.
- Increase staffing during peak workload periods.
- Monitor occupancy and discharge trends continuously.
- Improve coordination between CBP and HHS.
- Use real-time dashboards for operational monitoring.
